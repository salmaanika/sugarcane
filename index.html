About Sugarcane

Sugarcane leaf diseases are caused by various pathogens including fungi, bacteria, and viruses. These diseases can affect the photosynthetic ability of the plant, reduce yield, and impact sugar quality.

  • Healthy Sugarcane Leaf

    • Color: Uniform dark to medium green.
    • Texture: Smooth, glossy surface with no spots or streaks.
    • Shape: Straight, erect leaves with parallel venation.
    • Appearance: No signs of wilting, yellowing, or deformation.
    • Significance: Indicates proper nutrition, good plant health, and high productivity
  • Red Rot (Colletotrichum falcatum)

    • Cause: Fungal infection.
    • Symptoms:
    o Initially affects the stalk but can spread to leaves.
    o Leaves may dry from the tip downward (leaf drying starts from top).
    o Red discoloration with white patches in the internal tissues of stalks.
    • Spread: Through soil, infected plant debris, and tools.
  • Rust (Puccinia melanocephala – Brown Rust / Puccinia kuehnii – Orange Rust)

    • Cause: Fungal spores.
    • Symptoms:
    o Brown (or orange, depending on the type) pustules on the underside of leaves.
    o Yellowing or necrosis around the pustules.
    o Leaf drying and reduced photosynthesis.
    • Spread: Wind-borne spores.
  • Sugarcane Mosaic Disease

    • Cause: Virus (SCMV or SrMV).
    • Symptoms:
    o Mosaic pattern: alternating light green and dark green patches or streaks.
    o Leaf may appear mottled or speckled.
    o Leaf crinkling or mild distortion in severe cases.
    o Plant may be stunted with reduced vigor.
    • Spread: Mainly by aphids and infected planting material.
  • Yellow Leaf Disease (Sugarcane Yellow Leaf Virus - SCYLV)

    • Cause: Viral infection.
    • Symptoms:
    o Yellowing of the midrib and leaf blade, especially in young leaves.
    o Reduced tillering and stunted growth.
    o May remain symptomless in some varieties.
    • Spread: Aphids (especially Melanaphis sacchari).
